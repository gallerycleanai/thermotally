# ThermoTally: keyword, SERP & competitor research (Oct 25, 2026)

Free sources only: Google Autocomplete (US + UK), Google Trends (12 months, relative 0–100), live SERP samples, competitor pricing pages, App Store / Capterra reviews, RDAP (.com availability).
No paid keyword tool was used, so volumes are **relative**, not absolute. Search Console impressions in weeks 2–8 give the real numbers.

## 1. Headline findings
1. **The demand is a long tail, not a few big keywords.** Every single template phrase ("fridge temperature log sheet pdf", "food cooling log") scores 0–1 on Trends, but Autocomplete returns dozens of real variants per seed (pdf / printable / free download / template / for restaurant / for food truck / excel / book). The parent terms are healthy: US Trends `haccp` 71, `hot holding` 69, `temperature log` 31–44, `food safety app` 35, `food safety software` 21, `receiving log` 12–13.
2. **The SERPs are beatable.** Template queries are dominated by **government / health-department PDFs** (eohu.ca, publichealthmdc.com, health.mn.gov, VDH, RI DOH, NDDH, San Diego County, Texas DSHS, Maine, Montana DPHHS, ICN, NC State) and **small template sites** (rockpaperprint, foodhandlergear, haccplan, tooboringtowork, trainedteam, rotahr, binwise), plus FoodDocs' template pages. Bare PDFs have little on-page content, no FAQ and no schema, so an HTML landing page with a good PDF, a rules table and an FAQ can compete. FoodDocs is the only funded player doing template SEO seriously.
3. **App queries show buyer intent and weak incumbents.** "restaurant temperature log app" surfaces indie apps (Barley, Ordinoo, KitchenTemp, TempLog Pro); "food safety app for small restaurants" surfaces listicles (Hubl, foodaidaily), SafePlate, ChefComply and Chef Hygiene. No dominant brand owns the small-business term.
4. **US first, UK later (or never).** UK interest is real (GB Trends `temperature log` 43, `sfbb` 21; `food hygiene rating` 76 vs `haccp` 44), but the FSA's free *Safer Food, Better Business* pack and gov.uk own the top results, and UK price anchors are low (SFBB+ £4.99/mo, Forkto £13, Hubl £19, Trail £32–38). US pricing is far higher ($60–300/location) and the SERPs are fragmented across ~3,000 local health departments.
5. **Honest risk:** the cheap, simple digital temp-log niche got crowded in 2025–26 (LogChill $18–24, KitchenTemp $24, SafePlate **free** for 1 location, ChefComply $59.99, FreshCheq $60). The product alone is not a moat. **Distribution (template SEO) and vertical focus (trucks, cafés) have to be the edge**, and this test measures exactly that.

### Gaps for single-location restaurants, trucks, cafés and home bakers
1. **Price and contract shape:** incumbents are quote-only, annual or 24-month, with setup or hardware fees. Reviewers explicitly name **$20–40/month** as the fair price.
2. **Reliability on inspection day:** the Jolt outage review shows the fear is "my logs aren't there when the inspector is". That argues for offline-first, an always-available PDF and data export.
3. **Missed-check nudges:** the top feature request in micro-business reviews (SFBB+).
4. **Paper-first users:** nobody bridges the paper sheet to digital. A "photo of the filled sheet → filed + flagged" option is untested but unique; the waitlist asks about it.
5. **Food trucks:** offline use, commissary checklists and multi-county inspection are barely addressed. The 2026 Food Code adds mobile-food-establishment and commissary definitions.
6. **Home bakers / cottage food:** usually exempt from inspection under cottage laws, so this is a **weak** paying segment. Serve them with free templates only; target licensed cafés and bakeries instead.

## 4. Positioning (chosen)
> **"Food safety logs your team will actually fill in. For the one-location kitchen."**
> Restaurants, food trucks, cafés and bakeries. Log a reading in ~10 seconds, get a nudge when a check is missed, and print an inspector-ready PDF. Flat month-to-month price per location: no hardware, no setup fee, no annual contract. Free printable logs that match the app, so paper users can start free and switch later.

Wedge = SEO distribution through free, high-quality printable logs + vertical pages (trucks, cafés). The engine stays generic ("scheduled check + limit + corrective action + report"), so it can later cover other compliance logs (pool/spa chemistry, childcare, cannabis, pharmacy fridges, etc.).
